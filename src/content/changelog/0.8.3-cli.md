Research investigations and external materials get their own document types and relations. The vocabulary grows to 21 types and 7 relations.

## Improvements

- New `research` document type in the vision category. It maps a territory with Goal, Scope, Coverage, Sources, Findings, Synthesis, and Open Gaps, and closes on coverage of its declared scope rather than on a verdict. `rnd` keeps its scope as the investigation that ends in a recommendation.
- New `evidence` document type in the knowledge category. It records one external material with a Locator (address, access date, publication date, publisher), an Extract, and Notes. The frontmatter stays `title`, `status`, and `tags`.
- Relations gain `supports`, `contradicts`, and `supersedes`. `supports` and `contradicts` run from the material to the statement it backs or disputes; `supersedes` runs from the newer document to the older one. The three join the four structural types on every type pair. See [Relations](/concepts/relations/).
- An older CLI rejects a manifest that contains the new relation values. Every reader of a shared repository needs v0.8.3 before the first such edge lands.
- The server instructions carry the type catalog, three new type-selection rules (`rnd` vs `research`, `research` vs `doc`, `evidence` vs statement), the relation axes, and the status readings for both types.
- Relation tools reject global sources, absolute paths, and paths outside the project on either endpoint.
- `remove_relation` still removes relations stored by older binaries with uncleaned paths.
- Telemetry and installer beacons report to `edge.archcore.ai`.

## Fixes

- `update_document` preserves frontmatter keys outside `title`, `status`, and `tags` instead of dropping them.
