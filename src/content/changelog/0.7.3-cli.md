The precision advisory now measures a document against a written prose canon rather than a short lexicon. It reports where a statement sits in the wrong document type, and where the same statement sits in two.

## Improvements

- Each kind of content has one owning document type and one section inside it. A foreign-section finding names a heading whose content another type owns, and names that type. See [Content ownership](/concepts/document-types/#content-ownership).
- A restatement check compares the written document against the documents it links with `implements` and `extends`, and names a statement that survived the move nearly word for word. It reads at most 5 neighbours and reports at most 3 findings.
- The `prd` template is reduced to four sections: Vision, Problem Statement, Goals and Success Metrics, Requirements. A section the `prd` does not own is no longer offered to the author.
- Every document type carries a prose profile. STE binds a type whose lines instruct or obligate; ISO binds a type whose lines argue or describe. The profile decides which checks may report on the type.
- New findings grade requirements and steps: over 25 words, over 20 words, a condition stated after the obligation, an open-ended list, an ambiguous alternative, and a BCP 14 modal in a step or in a claim.
- New per-type findings cover a `rule` that names no file target, a `rule` whose Enforcement names no verifier, an `adr` with bulleted Context or a single alternative, an alternative recorded without a reason, a `cpat` whose Before or After holds no code block, and an EARS clause inside a `prd` requirement.
- One report carries at most 5 findings and ends with a count of what the cap dropped. [Precision checks](/reference/precision-checks/) lists all 27.
- `archcore hooks opencode <event>` serves OpenCode. OpenCode hooks are JavaScript plugins and cannot be declared in a config file, so Archcore ships the leaves for a plugin to delegate to and writes no wiring itself.

## Fixes

- The vagueness lexicon no longer reads a heading as a claim, so a `doc` template's own `## Best Practices` section stops reporting itself.
- A numbered clause spanning two source lines is measured once instead of having its second half read as prose.
- Fenced blocks are excluded from the clause and bullet checks. The Good and Bad examples a `rule` owes its reader are no longer counted as that rule's own requirements.
- A line opening with a decimal number is no longer read as a numbered clause. `1.25 or newer is required` was graded as clause 1.
